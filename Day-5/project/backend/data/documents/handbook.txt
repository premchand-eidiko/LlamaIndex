EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - ENTERPRISE POLICIES & GUIDELINES

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
Welcome to our company! We are dedicated to building cutting-edge enterprise AI solutions while fostering an inclusive, collaborative, and innovative workplace where every team member can thrive.

2. WORK FROM HOME & HYBRID WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week as part of our hybrid work model.
Remote work arrangements must be coordinated with the employee's direct manager and registered with the HR department.
High-speed internet stipends and home-office equipment allowances are provided upon onboarding.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE & TIME OFF POLICY
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave should be requested via the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.
Additional leave may be granted for medical reasons, family emergencies, bereavement, or special circumstances subject to manager approval.
Employees are encouraged to take their leave to maintain health and work-life balance.

4. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
We offer comprehensive benefits including:
- Top-tier medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage from day one.
- 401(k) retirement savings plan with 5% company matching.
- Annual $2,500 learning and professional development stipend for conferences, books, and courses.
- Wellness stipend covering gym memberships and fitness subscriptions.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT & ETHICS
All employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and mutual respect in all interactions with colleagues, customers, and partners.
Discussions regarding security vulnerabilities, confidential client data, and proprietary models must strictly adhere to confidentiality agreements and security protocols.
