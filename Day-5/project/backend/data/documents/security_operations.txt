SECURITY OPERATIONS & IT INFRASTRUCTURE MANUAL

1. LEADERSHIP & ROLES
Alice manages the Security Operations Team (SecOps) under the IT Operations department.
Alice oversees cyber defense, incident response, vulnerability management, and infrastructure compliance.
Bob serves as the Lead Security Architect, reporting directly to Alice.
The SecOps on-call hotline and escalation email is secops-alert@enterprise.com.

2. SECURITY ARCHITECTURE & POLICIES
The company enforces a strict Zero Trust Architecture across all cloud and on-premises environments.
Key policies implemented by the SecOps team include:
- Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all identity access points.
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) adhering to the principle of least privilege.
- Continuous vulnerability scanning and weekly patch verification.
- 24/7 security monitoring with automatic threat isolation.

3. INCIDENT RESPONSE WORKFLOW
In the event of a suspected security breach:
Step 1: Isolate affected endpoints immediately from the internal network.
Step 2: Notify Alice and the SecOps on-call lead within 15 minutes.
Step 3: Preserve forensic logs and begin triage according to NIST 800-61 standards.
Step 4: Conduct a post-incident review and deliver findings to senior management.
